# Music Space 🎸

Campfire guitar, in one app that works with no signal.

**Live:** https://feirzen.github.io/music-space/ (add it to your Home Screen)

- **Library:** songs in Sets that unlock as you nail them. Transpose, capo, "fit my range", autoscroll, tap any chord for its fingering, strum or fingerpick view, and a button to hear the original on Spotify.
- **Practice:** mic tuner, metronome, chord library, Chord Flash, Chord Rush and a shape quiz.
- **Path:** short Duolingo-style lessons with boss levels.

Built-in songs are public domain. Your own songs, PDFs and progress sync to the **private** repo `music-space-library` using a token that stays on your device.
